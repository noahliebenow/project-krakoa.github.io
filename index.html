PROJECT / KRAKOA

Work faster. Think deeper.

AI-assisted workflows for Rapid Intelligence.

Right now, Rapid Intelligence analysts spend too much of their day on work that doesn't require their best thinking. Sorting through content, roughing out first drafts—Project Krakoa is built to take that off their plate.

By handling the repetitive, time-consuming parts of the briefings workflow, the platform frees analysts to focus on what they're actually great at. Making smart editorial calls, surfacing insights that matter, delivering media intelligence their clients can act on.

This isn't about replacing analysts. It's about giving them better tools so their expertise shows up in the final product. Teams move faster, output gets sharper, and the work stops feeling like a grind.
